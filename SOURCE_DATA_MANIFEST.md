## Extended Figures

Extended figures are retained as
`outputs/appendix/extended_data_figure_*.{png,pdf}` and are generated by
`scripts_R/10_extended_data.R` plus the sourced extended-data scripts under
`scripts_R/`.

The main retained inputs are:

- `data/processed/country_profile_inputs.csv`
- `data/processed/country_contact_matrices_8groups.csv`
- `data/processed/pertussis_incidence_timeseries.csv`
- `data/raw/country_resistance_timeline.csv`
- `publication_inputs/scenario_table.csv`
- `publication_inputs/resistance_scenario_table.csv`
- `publication_inputs/fitness_grid_table.csv`
- `outputs/summaries/*.csv`
- `outputs/tables/*.csv`
- `outputs/metadata/*_run_metadata.json`
- `outputs/metadata/beta_grid_bayesian_uncertainty/*.csv`
- `outputs/calibrations/*.yaml`

The source-data layer keeps CSV tables and rendered figures. Parquet mirrors and
large local time-series outputs are omitted to keep the repository lightweight.
