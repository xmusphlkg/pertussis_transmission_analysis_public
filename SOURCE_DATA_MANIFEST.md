figures. Bulk simulation time series and parquet mirrors are regenerated by the
pipeline and are intentionally omitted from the lightweight public snapshot.

Independent resistance-management selected-input source data are retained in
`outputs/tables/resistance_management_psa_parameter_samples.csv`,
`outputs/tables/resistance_management_psa_effect_samples.csv`,
`outputs/summaries/resistance_management_psa_summary.csv`, and
`outputs/metadata/resistance_management_psa_run_metadata.json`. This separate
five-year, two-input design reports empirical design frequencies within restored
and not-restored PEP-effectiveness strata. It is not Figure 2b and is not a
source for Figure S10 unless an actual rendered panel is later wired to these
artifacts.

Extended figures are rendered by `scripts_R/extended_data/` and retained as
`outputs/appendix/extended_data_figure_*`. Their principal input layers are
`data/processed/`, the allowlisted computational files in `manuscript_notes/`, `outputs/tables/`,
`outputs/summaries/`, `outputs/calibrations/`, and `outputs/metadata/`.

Run metadata record configuration and source hashes, dependency versions, row
counts, and audited artifact digests. `ANALYSIS_PROTOCOL.md` defines which
artifacts belong to the current release and the claim limits attached to them.
